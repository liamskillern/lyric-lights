Privacy policy

Lyric Lights · effective November 23, 2026

Lyric Lights is a free visualizer for the music you play on Spotify. It's a personal project by liamskillern, and its full source code is public on GitHub.

The short version

  • There's no Lyric Lights server and no Lyric Lights account. The app runs on your device and talks straight to Spotify and LRCLIB.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Sound from your microphone or computer is analyzed live on your device and never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

What the app reads from Spotify

Only after you log in with Spotify and agree, the app reads what you're playing right now: the song, artist and album names, the cover art, where you are in the song, whether it's playing, and the next song in your queue. It asks Spotify for read-only access (user-read-currently-playing and user-read-playback-state), so it can't change your account, playlists or playback. This information is used only to draw the visuals on your screen.

Who else receives data

Service What it receives Why
Spotify Your login, and requests for what's playing and your queue To know what you're listening to. Spotify's privacy policy applies.
LRCLIB (lrclib.net) The song's title, artist, album and length To find time-synced lyrics. No account or personal information is sent.

These requests go directly from your device to those services. Lyric Lights never sees them on a server, because it doesn't have one.

Microphone and computer sound (optional)

If you turn on beat sync, the app listens to the microphone or, on a computer, to the sound you choose to share. The sound is measured live (loudness and beats) to move the visuals in time with the music. It's never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. When you share your screen to get computer sound, the picture is thrown away immediately and only the sound is used. Turning beat sync off, logging out or closing the app stops listening.

What's stored on your device

Item Where How long
Spotify access token App memory only Until the app is reloaded or closed
Spotify refresh token Session storage (this window only) Until you log out or close the app
Your Spotify Client ID and settings Local storage Until you clear the app's data or uninstall it

Logging out deletes your login from the device. To remove the app's access at Spotify too, go to spotify.com/account/apps.

The Android app

The Android app opens this same website full-screen in Google Chrome. Everything above applies to it. Chrome's own privacy policy covers the browser itself.

Children

Lyric Lights isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
